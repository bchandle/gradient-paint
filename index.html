∇ Gradient Paint

Gradient Paint

You paint gradients, not colours: every stroke edits the image's x- and y-derivatives, and a multigrid integrator running on the GPU turns them back into an image every frame. Strokes are directional — the colour goes on the left of the direction you draw, its complement on the right.

Brushes ↑ ↓

Paint
Left-drag paints an oriented edge in the current colour. Right-drag picks hue/saturation; Shift+right-drag picks value (−1…1; negative flips the edge).
Clone
Right-click sets the source, then left-drag copies gradients from there.
Edge
Right-drag along an edge in the image to capture it (Shift+right-click extends it), then left-drag to paint copies of it. The capture loops along long strokes.

Blend modes ← →

add
accumulates strokes — sketching, shading
dir
strengthens existing gradients pointing the brush's way — lighting, contrast
max
keeps the stronger gradient — cloning, copying edges
min
keeps the weaker gradient — smoothing, removing wrinkles
over
replaces gradients — cloning, erasing
